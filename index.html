Machining Physics Concept: Internal Boring vs Turning

Why did the bore get OVERSIZED?

In Internal Boring, any outward movement or radial growth of the boring bar tool tip pushes deeper into the aluminum workpiece wall, creating a larger bore diameter. Thermal expansion of the steel boring bar as coolant temperature rises is the primary driver!

The Tool Wear Paradox Trap

Normal insert flank wear causes tool length to shrink inward, which would make an internal bore undersized. Because this batch turned oversized (+35 to +48 µm), tool wear is NOT the main cause! Thermal growth + Built-Up Edge (BUE) are the true culprits.
